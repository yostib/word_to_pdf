Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Word to PDF Converter service collects, uses, and protects data when users upload documents to convert.

1. Information Collected

We collect only the information necessary to operate the service and provide conversion results.

  • Uploaded document files (.docx, .doc).
  • IP address for analytics and abuse prevention.
  • Temporary system data required for conversion and queue handling.

2. Use of Information

Information is used solely to:

  • Perform file conversions.
  • Maintain service availability.
  • Prevent abuse and enforce usage limits.
  • Provide admin analytics.

3. Data Retention

Uploaded documents are deleted automatically after conversion. We do not retain user files longer than necessary to complete the requested conversion.

4. Analytics

Basic analytics are collected to measure service usage and performance, such as total conversions, daily totals, and queue status. Analytics are stored in a local JSON file and used for internal reporting only.

5. Cookies and Session Data

The admin dashboard uses secure session cookies to authenticate the administrator. No user-facing cookies are required for document conversion.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share uploaded files or personal data with third parties, except as required by law.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data from unauthorized access and to secure the conversion process. However, no system is completely secure, so use the service at your own risk.

8. Third-Party Services

The conversion process may depend on installed software such as LibreOffice. The service does not transmit uploaded documents to external cloud conversion providers.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The latest version will be available on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about privacy or data handling, contact the website administrator through the admin portal.
